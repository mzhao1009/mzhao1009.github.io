How does a white-forced fluid mix when the energy is infinite and the domain is unbounded?

My latest preprint proves uniqueness of the invariant measure and polynomial mixing for infinite-energy solutions of the white-forced Navier–Stokes equation in an infinite pipe, answering an open problem of Kuksin and Shirikyan in the zero-flux case.

About

Dynamics shaped
by randomness and
physical boundaries.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at CY Cergy Paris Université. I received my PhD in Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research asks how stochastic forcing changes nonlinear PDEs: whether it regularizes dynamics, how it reshapes invariant statistics, and what remains when viscosity vanishes. Boundaries and unbounded domains are recurring themes—not technical afterthoughts, but part of the mechanism.
